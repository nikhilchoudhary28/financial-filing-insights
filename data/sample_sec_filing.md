# Tesla, Inc. Annual Report on Form 10-K

## Business Overview

Tesla, Inc. designs, manufactures, and sells electric vehicles, energy generation and storage systems, and related services. We believe our business continues to benefit from strong vehicle demand, improvements in manufacturing efficiency, and disciplined operating expense management.

## Consolidated Statements of Operations

| Metric | 2024 | 2023 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Total revenue | $97.7 billion | $96.8 billion |
| Gross profit | $19.9 billion | $18.7 billion |
| Operating income | $7.6 billion | $7.1 billion |
| Net income | $7.1 billion | $5.6 billion |
| Adjusted operating margin | 8.6% | 7.6% |

## Liquidity and Capital Resources

Our balance sheet includes total assets of $128.7 billion and total liabilities of $81.3 billion, with cash, cash equivalents, and investments of $29.3 billion. Automotive revenue was approximately $78.4 billion in 2024 and energy generation and storage revenue was approximately $10.4 billion.

## Risk Factors

Key risks include intense competition, supply chain constraints, pricing pressure, regulatory change, and the need to successfully ramp production of new vehicles and energy products.

## Management Discussion

We expect future growth to be driven by continued production scale, software and services, and our energy business. We also expect operating leverage to improve as manufacturing efficiency continues to rise and fixed costs are spread across higher unit volumes.
